-[] interface
    -[] review-async
        Generates an audio file to listen to to study while doing other things
        --reverse, -r
            play the content and then the card title
        --shuffle, -s
            play cards in a random order
        --answer-time n
            Amount of time in seconds between reading the question and reading the answer
    -[] review
        Displays cards one at a time on screen and waits for the answer
        --reverse
            play the card content and then the title
        --shuffle
            play cards in random order
        --tts
            enable text-to-speech for reading out card titles and answers
    -[] init
        Turns a folder of card-like files into a deck of flash cards
    -[] info
        Prints info about the current deck
